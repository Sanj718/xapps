Get up and running with Section Conditions + on your Shopify store.

## Installing the App

1. Visit the app on the [Shopify App Store](https://apps.shopify.com/section-conditions-plus)
2. Click **Install** and authorize the required permissions
3. You'll be redirected to the app dashboard inside your Shopify admin

[[SCREENSHOT: App dashboard home showing the rules list and the 3-step getting started guide]]

## Getting Started

After installation, follow the 3-step setup guide on your dashboard:

### Step 1: Activate the App Embed

Open your theme editor and enable the **Section Conditions +** app embed under **App embeds**. This is required for rules to take effect on your storefront — without it, no sections will be shown or hidden.

[[SCREENSHOT: Shopify theme editor with the Section Conditions + app embed toggle highlighted under App embeds]]

### Step 2: Create Your First Rule

Click **Create rule**, pick a section from your storefront using the visual section picker, then set the conditions that control when that section appears or is hidden.

[[SCREENSHOT: Rule form showing a section selected, action set to "Hide if", and a sample condition]]

### Step 3: Preview on Your Storefront

Visit your store to see the rule in action. Rules evaluate the moment the page loads, and cart-based conditions re-check automatically as the cart changes — no reload needed for those. Location, customer, and page conditions are read once per page load, so a change like logging in mid-session needs a page reload to be picked up.
